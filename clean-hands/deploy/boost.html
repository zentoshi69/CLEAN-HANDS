Spot checks

Boosting, answered clean

Is my CLEAN ever custodied?
No. Staking is soft — tokens stay in your wallet. Liquidity is provided directly to the pool from your wallet, and burns are on-chain transactions you sign. Nothing is ever held by us.
What does “additive” mean for boosters?
Each lever (holdings, loyalty, referrals, liquidity) adds a fixed amount to your booster — they don’t multiply each other. Maxing all four reaches a 3× multiplier on your base APR. Burning then adds flat bonus APR on top of that.
How does the liquidity (MM) booster work?
Add $CLEAN + SOL to the market-maker pool and you earn up to +0.70× — the tier that tops your stack to 3×. You collect a share of real swap fees and carry real impermanent-loss risk, like any LP.
Is burning really permanent?
Yes. Burns are irreversible on-chain transactions that shrink supply for everyone and permanently raise your APR (+0.05 per 100k, up to +200%). The bonus never decays.

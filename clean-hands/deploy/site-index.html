Questions, answered clean

$CLEAN is a community meme token built around one joke: clean hands, dirty money. This site lets holders stake into spin cycles and burn $CLEAN to boost their yield. It's a meme — have fun, but never stake more than you can afford to lose.

You burn $CLEAN to raise your boost multiplier (up to 2.5×), applied on top of the cycle's base APY. Burned tokens are gone for good, so every boost permanently shrinks supply — good for the boosters and for everyone holding.

No. "Washing" and "laundering" here are pure theme and wordplay. Staking just locks tokens in a contract to earn yield. Nothing illegal is happening, and nothing here is financial advice.

Always verify audits and contract addresses yourself before connecting a wallet to any protocol. The live contract address is pinned on this site (CA chip) — always verify it yourself. Staking runs in the official CLEAN app at app.cleanhands.fun.

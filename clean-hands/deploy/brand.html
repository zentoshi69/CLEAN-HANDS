Who we are

Essence & story

CLEAN is a soft-staking memecoin on Solana with one joke and one promise: money is dirty, so we keep it spotless. The whole brand is a laundromat for your bags — bright, premium, a little cheeky, and ruthlessly honest (non-custodial, on-chain, nothing hidden).

Mission

Make on-chain yield feel clean — transparent mechanics, your keys, no rug residue.

Personality

Playful but premium. Meme energy with a tailored shirt. Confident, witty, never sleazy.

The metaphor

Washing. Gloves on, soap bubbles, spin cycle, come out fresh. Every word and pixel leans in.

The mark

Logo & mascot

The hero asset is the glove 🧤 — a clean blue-rubber dishwashing glove, rounded into a coin. The signature accent is the four-point sparkle ✦, used as bullet, flourish, and "freshly cleaned" stamp.

Clear space & minimums

Clear space = at least ½ the glove's diameter on every side.

Min size 28px (favicon) · 38–46px (nav) · 96–120px (hero).

Always on a circle with a 1.5px --line ring on light surfaces.

Don'ts

Never

  • Stretch, skew, or rotate the glove
  • Recolour it outside the sky/ink palette
  • Add drop-shadows or outer glows to the mark itself
  • Place it on busy photos without the white circle

Always

  • Keep it circular, centred, calm
  • Pair with Fraunces wordmark + Caveat sub
  • Let the ✦ do the sparkle, not the logo
  • Give it room to breathe

Typography

Three voices: a couture serif for display, a clean grotesque for everything functional, and a marker scrawl for personality.

Look & feel

Imagery

Bright, airy, slightly surreal. Think detergent-ad cleanliness crossed with crypto. Subjects: gloves, soap suds, washing machines, banknotes going in dirty and coming out gleaming, blue skies and water.

Do

  • High-key lighting, soft shadows, lots of white space
  • Sky-blue + white world, pops of the green/purple accents
  • Glossy bubbles, water droplets, sparkle highlights
  • Rounded crops; place photos in 22px-radius frames

Don't

  • Dark, gritty, "degen dungeon" aesthetics
  • Harsh stock-photo handshakes or cash fans
  • Cluttered charts as decoration
  • Realistic gore/grime — keep "dirty" playful

The ✦ system

The four-point sparkle is the connective tissue: bullet points, end-of-line flourishes ("Copied ✦"), reward confirmations, and the burst particle. Variants: ✦ ✧ ✶ ✷. Use one or two per view — sparkle is a spice.

How we talk

Voice & tone

Witty, warm, and disarmingly honest. We make the joke and tell you exactly how the money works. Short sentences. Active verbs. The wash metaphor, always.

Sounds like

  • "Wash your bags. Keep your keys. Come out spotless."
  • "Soft-staking — your tokens never leave your wallet."
  • "Burn to boost. Permanent, on-chain, deflationary."
  • "Clean hands, dirty money 🧤"

Never sounds like

  • "GUARANTEED 1000x APY!!! 🚀🚀"
  • Corporate filler / whitepaper-speak walls
  • Fear-of-missing-out pressure or fake scarcity
  • Mean-spirited or punching-down jokes

# CLEAN Hands Staking One-Pager

Audience: users, partners, and moderators who need a fast explanation of CLEAN
soft staking and boosters.

Core message:

> PLAY & STAKE `$CLEAN`. Connect your Solana wallet, soft-stake what you already
> hold, and grow staking power with verified boosters. Tokens never leave your
> wallet when staking.

## Mechanism

1. **Connect wallet** - sign a one-time login message. No custody, no spending
   approval.
2. **Soft stake** - enroll a percent of your live `$CLEAN` wallet balance.
3. **Earn rewards** - rewards accrue from the server-side economics engine.
4. **Boost staking power** - holdings, loyalty, referrals, wallet balance,
   liquidity, Escape game, socials, VIP, and burns can increase rewards.
5. **Claim later** - rewards unlock after the continuous staking window and are
   requested as manual treasury payouts.

## Booster rules

| Booster | Activation | Default effect |
|---|---|---|
| Holdings | Stake more `$CLEAN` | up to +0.50x |
| Loyalty | Stay staked | +0.05x per 30 days, cap +0.50x |
| Referrals | Invite active stakers | +0.02x each, cap +0.30x |
| Wallet balance | Hold qualifying SOL/CLEAN | +0.10x to +0.50x |
| Liquidity | Verified MM deposit | up to +0.70x |
| Escape game | Verified x5/x10/x20/x33 score | +20% / +33% / +50% / +100% |
| Socials | TG + X + Discord | activates 1/3 of Escape each |
| Burn | Burn `$CLEAN` on-chain | +5% APR per 100k, cap +200% APR |

## Safety

- Rewards use `min(staked, live wallet balance)`.
- Raw game saves do not pay - Escape uses a hidden verification ledger.
- X/Discord do not self-unlock - they require server/admin verification.
- Burns and MM deposits are verified on-chain and credited once.
- Claims require the live balance check and create manual payout requests.
